V42.8.1 - TINH GON VA CHUAN HOA MENU 16
- Doi ten menu 16 thanh LUYEN NGHE PHIEN AM TIENG TRUNG; tang do dam mau nen menu va de so 16 mau den dong bo voi cac menu con lai.
- Tieu de chuc nang ben phai dong bo voi ten menu 16.
- Muc 16.1 gom bang cac phien am tieng Trung theo dung ma tran PDF: 23 hang thanh mau x 36 cot van mau. Giu nguyen cac o co am tiet va cac o trong (391 am tiet).
- Muc 16.1 co dung 6 nut chuc nang: Thanh 1, Thanh 2, Thanh 3, Thanh 4, Doc thanh mau, Doc van mau.
- Khi bam mot o am tiet, o duoc danh dau dang duoc chon va phat ban ghi am cua am tiet theo thanh dang chon. Chu Pinyin tren bang hien thi dau thanh chuan tren nguyen am.
- Hai nut Doc thanh mau / Doc van mau phat lan luot cac mau am tiet bang cung bo ban ghi am. Cac mau doc thanh mau duoc chuan lai: b/bo, p/po, m/mo, f/fo, d/de, t/te, n/ne, l/le.
- Bo cac nut loa va sao chep rieng tung o; khong su dung SpeechSynthesis/TTS mac dinh cua iPhone, Android hoac Windows. Am thanh duoc phat tu tep ghi am co san.
- Muc 16.2 la bai luyen nghe ngau nhien. So cau moi luot cho phep nhap tu do (mac dinh 5); co the tra loi rieng thanh dieu, rieng am tiet Pinyin khong dau, hoac ca hai. Ket qua hien thi so cau dung, ty le dung va thoi gian hoan thanh.
- Bo giao dien ghi chu phu, nhan A/B/C, nhan so dem 391 am tiet, nut sao chep va cac nut loa rieng le trong bang.
- Service Worker luu tung tep ghi am vao cache khi duoc phat; cac tep da duoc phat co the tiep tuc dung ngoai tuyen tren cung thiet bi neu Service Worker da kiem soat trang.
